# Typewriter Astrology Content Analysis

Last updated: 2026-07-04

This file analyzes Typewriter Astrology as a content reference for Mystic Birth Chart.
Use it as a strategic model, not as a source to copy. Mystic should borrow the editorial logic:
timely astrology, sign-by-sign usefulness, dense but readable educational slides, and clear product funnels.
Do not copy Typewriter Astrology's exact wording, post titles, layouts, brand marks, or creator voice.

## What Is Working

### 1. Timely Astrology, Not Generic Astrology

Typewriter does not only post evergreen concepts like "what your Moon sign means."
They use timing:

- planet enters sign;
- retrograde periods;
- new and full moons;
- yearly transit themes;
- sign-by-sign/rising-sign breakdowns;
- monthly paid forecasts.

This makes the content feel current and useful. People have a reason to save, share, comment, and return.

Mystic should adopt this category.

Recommended Mystic content line:

```text
Transit Notes from the Old Study
```

Post subjects:

- "Mercury enters [sign]: what changes in speech, memory, and decisions"
- "Venus enters [sign]: what becomes desirable, costly, or pleasurable"
- "Mars enters [sign]: where pressure, motion, and conflict collect"
- "Jupiter in [sign]: where growth can become excess"
- "Saturn in [sign]: where form, delay, and mastery are required"
- "New Moon in [sign]: where the month begins again"
- "Full Moon in [sign]: what becomes visible"

Do not guess transit dates. Before publishing any dated transit post, verify the date with a current ephemeris.

### 2. Rising-Sign Utility

The "Aries risings in 2026" and "Taurus risings in 2026" examples work because they translate big sky events into personal house topics.

The format is strong:

```text
[Sign] risings in [year]

Where they are lucky/protected,
but also indulgent/overpromising:
[house topics]

Where they are sensitive/maturing/mastering,
but also meeting restriction/burdens:
[house topics]

Where there is experimentation/change,
but also instability/rebellion:
[house topics]

Where there are wounds/vulnerability,
but also healing/transformation:
[house topics]
```

This gives the reader a quick personal map. It is not just vibes.

Mystic should adapt this into a more traditional, old-study version:

```text
[Sign] Rising: 2026 House Notes

Where Jupiter opens a door:
[house topics]

Where Saturn asks for form:
[house topics]

Where Uranus unsettles the pattern:
[house topics]

Where Chiron names the tender place:
[house topics]

Read this through your full chart, not as a standalone fate.
```

Important:

- This should be based on whole-sign houses by rising sign.
- Always include a disclaimer that the full natal chart modifies the transit.
- This is excellent for carousels, Pinterest, and email capture.

### 3. Dense Slides That Reward Saving

The Typewriter examples use more text than Mystic's first posts.
That matters because the content feels useful enough to save.

Mystic's first launch posts were aesthetically stronger than the first rejected drafts, but the content was closer to a brand manifesto.
For ongoing growth, posts need more concrete material:

- dates;
- house topics;
- sign-specific notes;
- transit meanings;
- placement breakdowns;
- examples;
- "if this is your rising sign" applications.

The next Mystic posts should generally be 8-15 slides, not only 6.

Recommended slide density:

- Title slide: 12-20 words.
- Teaching slides: 35-70 words each.
- Sign/rising slides: 45-90 words each.
- Avoid tiny text below 28 px in 1080x1350 or 1080x1080 assets.
- Use high-contrast ink on parchment.

### 4. A Clear Funnel Behind the Content

Typewriter uses content to push:

- comments;
- guide unlocks;
- website membership;
- annual guide;
- custom reports;
- email access.

Mystic needs this same funnel logic.

For now, because the Instagram desktop link field is blocked, use:

```text

```

Once the mobile app link is added, use:

```text

```

Later, when automation is available:

```text

```

Do not use comment-gated offers until there is a manual or automated workflow to answer comments quickly.

## Differences Mystic Should Keep

Mystic should not become a Typewriter clone.

Keep:

- old wooden study atmosphere;
- chart-as-a-whole method;
- traditional-first language;
- Hermetic astrology only when tied to planets, decans, timing, houses, signs, and natal chart structure;
- more explicit sales path to Basic and Complete natal readings;
- warmer "old study" positioning.

Avoid:

- exact typewriter logo/monogram behavior;
- exact parchment-only page styling;
- copying captions;
- "comment guide" if no guide/automation exists yet;
- posting yearly claims without ephemeris verification.

## Mystic Content Pillars Inspired By This Analysis

### Pillar 1: Transit Notes

Purpose:

Use current sky events to make Mystic feel alive and timely.

Best formats:

- 1 event per post.
- 8-10 slides.
- Explain the planet, sign, traditional condition, house application, and CTA.

Example carousel:

```text
Slide 1:
Mercury enters Cancer.

Slide 2:
Mercury is the planet of speech, memory, messages, trade, learning, and interpretation.

Slide 3:
Cancer is lunar, protective, indirect, and memory-rich. Mercury here does not speak like a straight line. It speaks through feeling, history, family, and instinct.

Slide 4:
This can be good for journaling, private conversations, remembering old stories, repairing family language, or writing from emotional truth.

Slide 5:
The shadow is defensive speech: saying the feeling without naming the need, protecting the past too tightly, or confusing memory with evidence.

Slide 6:
For your chart, find Cancer. That house shows where messages, decisions, and mental weather are moving.

Slide 7:
If Cancer is your 10th house, this can touch public voice and career decisions. If it is your 4th, it may speak through home, family, privacy, and roots.

Slide 8:
This is why a transit is never generic. It lands somewhere specific in your chart.

Slide 9:
Start with the free chart snapshot at mysticbirthchart.com.
```

### Pillar 2: Rising-Sign House Forecasts

Purpose:

Give people personal-feeling content that is still educational.

Best formats:

- 12-slide carousel, one slide per rising sign; or
- 15-slide carousel: intro + 12 signs + method + CTA.

Example:

```text
Title:
Where Jupiter in Leo falls by rising sign

Slide structure for each rising:
Aries Rising: Leo is your 5th house.
Jupiter grows themes of pleasure, children, creativity, romance, games, performance, and personal joy. The promise is more vitality. The caution is excess, pride, or overpromising through pleasure.
```

Use this structure for:

- Jupiter sign changes;
- Saturn sign changes;
- Mars sign changes;
- Venus retrogrades;
- Mercury retrogrades;
- eclipses;
- lunations.

### Pillar 3: Planet Columns

Purpose:

This mirrors the "Newspaper column by Mercury signs" concept but gives Mystic its own editorial container.

Mystic series name ideas:

- "Letters from the Old Study"
- "The Planetary Column"
- "The Reading Table"
- "Notes by the Planet Signs"

Example concepts:

- Mercury signs: how the mind writes.
- Venus signs: how desire bargains.
- Mars signs: how the will moves.
- Moon signs: where the body remembers.
- Saturn signs: where maturity becomes form.

Recommended carousel structure:

```text
Slide 1:
The Planetary Column: Mercury Signs

Slide 2:
Mercury is not just communication. It is how the mind sorts, names, trades, remembers, and translates experience.

Slides 3-14:
One sign per slide.

Slide 15:
The sign is only one layer. The house, aspects, condition, and chart ruler decide how Mercury actually works in your life.
```

Example Mystic writing:

```text
Mercury in Aries:
The mind moves by ignition. It learns by beginning, arguing, testing, and saying the thing before it becomes polished. Its gift is courage of speech. Its risk is mistaking speed for clarity.
```

```text
Mercury in Taurus:
The mind trusts what it can test, repeat, touch, and keep. It may speak slowly, but the words can become durable. Its gift is retention. Its risk is refusing a new thought because the old one still feels safe.
```

### Pillar 4: Chart Method Posts

Purpose:

Differentiate Mystic from meme accounts and free calculators.

Topics:

- "Your chart is not a list of placements."
- "The Ascendant is the door, not the costume."
- "The chart ruler is where the story starts moving."
- "A planet's house matters as much as its sign."
- "Dignity is condition, not moral worth."
- "Aspects show relationship, not isolated traits."
- "Sect changes how planets behave."

These can be shorter than transit posts, but should still include examples.

### Pillar 5: Hermetic Astrology With Boundaries

Purpose:

Use the owner's real interest in Hermetic material without confusing the algorithm or drifting away from astrology.

Allowed post types:

- decan angels by zodiacal decan;
- planetary days/hours;
- planetary prayer/devotion tied to natal chart;
- Mars/Saturn/Jupiter etc. as inner discipline or formation;
- Arbatel framed through planetary astrology;
- Golden Dawn correspondences tied to planets/signs/decans.

Structure:

```text
Slide 1:
Planetary magic begins with the chart.

Slide 2:
The point is not random occult decoration. A practice becomes serious when it is tied to planet, sign, house, dignity, timing, and purpose.

Slide 3:
Before working with Venus, ask: where is Venus in the natal chart? What house does it rule? What aspects does it receive? Is Venus helped or pressured?
```

Realistic promise:

- confidence;
- joy;
- intuition;
- discipline;
- patience;
- courage;
- clarity;
- devotion;
- inner steadiness.

Avoid:

- guaranteed money;
- guaranteed love;
- guaranteed healing;
- fear-based spirit claims;
- medical/financial/legal outcomes.

## Visual Direction For New Posts

The current Mystic visual system is usable, but future posts should evolve:

- More parchment surface, less oversized empty card.
- More text per slide, but keep readability.
- Use old newspaper/field-note logic.
- Use small engraved illustrations sparingly.
- Use consistent section labels:
  - Transit Note
  - Rising Sign Notes
  - Planetary Column
  - Old Study Method
  - Hermetic Astrology
- Keep `mysticbirthchart.com` tiny but readable, not dominant.
- Avoid fake wood texture and overdone occult geometry.

Recommended asset formats:

- Instagram square: 1080x1080 for feed consistency.
- Instagram carousel text-heavy: 1080x1350 may improve readability, but check profile grid crop.
- Pinterest: 1000x1500 or 1080x1920.

## Production Rule

Before creating a dated transit post:

1. Verify the exact date and time from a current ephemeris.
2. Confirm the zodiac system being used.
3. Decide if the post is for all signs, rising signs, or one specific placement.
4. Write the concrete house-topic application.
5. Add a clear CTA to the free chart tool or reading.

## Bottom Line

Mystic's first social posts were good for establishing a serious brand tone.
The next phase needs more utility.

The Typewriter lesson is:

- People save posts that tell them what is happening now.
- People share posts that feel personally specific.
- People buy when the free content proves the astrologer can synthesize.

Mystic should move from manifesto posts to calendar-aware, rising-sign-aware, placement-aware educational content.
