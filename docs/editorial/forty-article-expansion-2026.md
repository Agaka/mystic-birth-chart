# Forty-Article Editorial Expansion - July 2026

This file is the production map and factual checklist for the 40-article expansion requested on 2026-07-11. It is an internal editorial document, not article copy.

## Editorial standard

- Target 1,100-1,700 useful words per article.
- Write in English for an international audience paying in USD.
- Explain historical context before making modern interpretive applications.
- Clearly distinguish Jewish Kabbalah, historical Hermetism, Renaissance occult philosophy, and modern Hermetic Qabalah.
- Never claim that an old source teaches a correspondence that belongs to a later system.
- Keep astrology non-fatalistic: describe cycles, pressures, choices, and areas of attention, not guaranteed events.
- Every article must connect the subject to reading a whole natal chart.
- Current-transit dates must be checked against a tropical geocentric ephemeris.
- Do not send birth data or personal details to analytics.
