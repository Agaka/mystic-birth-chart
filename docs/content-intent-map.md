## Internal-Link Priorities

1. Informational basics -> `/free-birth-chart`.
2. Buying/process content -> `/sample-report` and `/birth-chart-report`.
3. Broad natal synthesis content -> `/complete-natal-chart-reading`.
4. Love, career, timing, and compatibility clusters -> their matching commercial landing.
5. Every article may include one contextual mid-article CTA and one final CTA, but not a stack of unrelated products.

## Intent Labels

- Informational: teaches a concept or answers a question.
- Commercial investigation: compares methods, formats, or buying decisions.
- Transactional: tool, product, or checkout intent.
- Supporting: narrows a pillar topic and links upward.
