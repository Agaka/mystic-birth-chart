# Mystic Birth Chart: 12-Week Organic Growth Strategy

Last updated: 2026-07-10

This plan supports the core funnel:

`Traffic -> Free Chart Preview -> Essential Reading ($17) -> Complete Reading ($97)`

It does not authorize external publishing. All public assets must remain in natural English, use the studio identity, avoid fake proof or urgency, and preserve the distinction between the automated Essential reading and the individually prepared Complete reading.

## Channel Priority

1. Site and SEO: durable intent capture and the primary conversion surface.
2. Pinterest: evergreen visual search linked to articles, the Free Chart, and commercial landings.
3. YouTube long-form: trust, explanation, and searchable education.
4. The Reading Room Letters / Substack: retention after value is delivered.
5. Instagram: discovery, recognizable editorial style, and carousel education.
6. Reddit: useful participation and research, never a link-distribution channel.

## Weekly Content Engine

Each weekly theme becomes:

- 1 article or commercial landing page;
- 1 long-form YouTube video;
- 2 YouTube Shorts;
- 2 Instagram Reels;
- 1 Instagram carousel;
- 4-6 Pinterest Pins;
- 1 Reading Room Letter / Substack note;
- 1-2 useful Reddit discussions or answers.

The weekly source document is the article outline. Video, carousel, email, and social assets adapt the idea; they do not duplicate the article word for word.

## Editorial Distribution

- 50% felt problems: Sun-sign mismatch, Moon/Rising confusion, repeating relationships, feeling stuck, and career direction.
- 30% education: chart ruler, sect, houses and birth time, Venus sign versus house, and planetary condition.
- 20% bottom of funnel: free chart versus reading, Essential versus Complete, delivery, and the sample report.
- Hermetic Astrology, Kabbalah, and angelic material remain a separate chart-led editorial line. They may support specialist content but must not dominate the Home page or the core natal-reading funnel.

## UTM Convention

Use lowercase, hyphenated values and never put personal or birth data in a URL.

```text
utm_source=pinterest|youtube|instagram|substack|reddit
utm_medium=organic-social|video|email|community
utm_campaign=2026-q3-<theme>
```

Add `utm_content=<asset-slug>` only in publishing tools that need creative-level comparison. GA4 must receive campaign attribution only, never names, email addresses, dates, times, cities, coordinates, signs, chart results, or report text.

## Pinterest Production Notes

- Use 1000x1500 only when the asset pipeline is ready.
- Keep text legible on mobile and use one promise per Pin.
- Link each Pin to the most specific matching page, not always the Home page.
- Use 2-3 visual variants per winning title before inventing more topics.
- Do not include the Vercel URL in images. Use `mysticbirthchart.com` only.

## YouTube Production Notes

- Long-form target: one clear question, 8-14 minutes, with the Free Chart as the primary description link.
- Open with the problem in plain language, teach the traditional structure, show one hypothetical example, state limitations, then use one CTA.
- Shorts should answer one question completely; they should not end in an empty cliffhanger.
- AI voice and generated visuals may assist production, but scripts must remain accurate, sober, and consistent with the faceless studio identity.

## Reading Room Letters / Substack Notes

- Send one strong weekly letter or two shorter notes, not automatic full-article duplicates.
- Use an original introduction, one teaching point, one example, and one link back to the site.
- Keep subscription optional and separate from checkout consent.
- Export subscriber data periodically if the provider supports it.

## Reddit Rules

- No mass posting, multiple accounts, repeated links, simulated testimonials, or disguised promotion.
- Read each community's rules before participating.
- Prefer useful answers without links.
- When a self-owned link genuinely answers the question, disclose the relationship to Mystic Birth Chart.
- Never use vulnerable personal stories as sales opportunities.

## Weekly Measurement

Review every Monday:

- organic impressions and clicks by landing page;
- Free Chart starts and completions;
- Essential and Complete CTA clicks;
- checkout starts and confirmed purchases;
- Pinterest outbound clicks and saves;
- YouTube click-through rate, average view duration, and description-link clicks;
- email opt-ins, opens, clicks, and unsubscribes;
- Instagram profile visits and site clicks;
- Reddit referral sessions and assisted conversions, without tracking individuals.

Do not declare a content winner from likes alone. A useful winner either grows qualified search demand, completes the Free Chart funnel, earns email consent, or contributes to a verified purchase.
