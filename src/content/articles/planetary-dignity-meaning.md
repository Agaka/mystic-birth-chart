## Not Every Placement Works the Same Way

Planetary dignity is one of the traditional techniques that makes astrology more precise.

It asks whether a planet is in a sign where it can act with strength, comfort, tension, or difficulty. This does not make the person good or bad. It describes how much support the planet has to do its job.

Without dignity, all sign placements can start to sound equal. Traditional astrology does not treat them that way.

## Domicile

A planet is in domicile when it is in a sign it rules.

Mars is at home in Aries and Scorpio. Venus is at home in Taurus and Libra. Mercury is at home in Gemini and Virgo. Jupiter is at home in Sagittarius and Pisces. Saturn is at home in Capricorn and Aquarius. The Sun is at home in Leo. The Moon is at home in Cancer.

A planet in domicile has authority. It can act more directly according to its own nature.

## Exaltation

Exaltation is another form of strength, but it is different from domicile.

An exalted planet is honored. It may act with visibility, refinement, or special effectiveness, though sometimes with a heightened quality.

For example, the Sun is exalted in Aries, the Moon in Taurus, Venus in Pisces, Mars in Capricorn, Jupiter in Cancer, Saturn in Libra, and Mercury in Virgo.

Exaltation can show strong capacity, but it still needs context.

## Detriment and Fall

Detriment happens when a planet is opposite a sign it rules. Fall happens when a planet is opposite its exaltation.

These conditions can describe difficulty, awkwardness, overcompensation, or the need to work harder to express the planet constructively.

This does not mean the placement is doomed. Difficult dignity can produce skill because the person must become conscious of the planet.

Venus in Virgo, for example, is in fall. That does not mean a person cannot love. It may show that love, pleasure, and value are filtered through discernment, improvement, service, or anxiety.

## Why Dignity Matters in Readings

Dignity helps the reader avoid generic interpretation.

Mars in Capricorn and Mars in Cancer do not operate the same way. Venus in Taurus and Venus in Scorpio do not have the same resources. Saturn in Libra and Saturn in Aries do not carry pressure in the same style.

If a planet rules important houses, its dignity becomes even more important. A challenged chart ruler may describe a life theme that requires more effort and integration.

## Dignity Is Not Destiny

Planetary dignity is descriptive, not fatalistic. A strong planet can be misused. A difficult planet can become mature and skilled.

The question is not whether a placement is good or bad. The question is how the planet functions and what kind of support it has.
