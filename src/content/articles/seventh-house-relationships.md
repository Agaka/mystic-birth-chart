## The House Across From You

The 7th house sits opposite the 1st house. The 1st house describes the self, body, identity, and approach to life. The 7th describes the other: partners, spouses, clients, collaborators, open enemies, and people who meet us directly.

This is why the 7th house is central in relationship astrology.

But it should not be reduced to romance alone. The 7th house describes one-to-one encounter. It shows how we meet another person across the table.

## Planets in the 7th House

Planets in the 7th house become visible through relationships.

Venus in the 7th may bring attraction, harmony, beauty, and partnership themes into focus. Mars in the 7th may bring passion, conflict, pursuit, or direct confrontation. Saturn in the 7th may bring seriousness, delay, commitment, fear, or responsibility in partnership.

The Moon in the 7th can show emotional mirroring through relationships. The Sun in the 7th may show identity developing through direct engagement with others.

The planet becomes part of the relationship field.

## The Ruler of the 7th House

An empty 7th house does not mean no partnership. The ruler of the 7th tells the story.

If the 7th house begins in Taurus, Venus rules partnership. If it begins in Scorpio, Mars rules partnership in traditional astrology. If it begins in Pisces, Jupiter rules partnership.

Then the reader studies that planet by sign, house, aspects, and condition.

This is where traditional astrology becomes more useful than simple compatibility lists.

## What the 7th House Can Reveal

The 7th house can show:

- What kind of people you attract
- What you seek in partnership
- What you project onto others
- What relationship asks you to develop
- Where commitment may feel easy or difficult
- How direct conflict operates

It can also show patterns with clients and collaborators, especially for people whose work depends on one-to-one service.

## The 7th House and Projection

Because the 7th house sits opposite the Ascendant, it can show qualities we meet through others before fully owning them ourselves.

A person with Aries on the 7th may encounter assertive or direct partners. A person with Capricorn on the 7th may meet serious, older, responsible, or guarded partners. A person with Gemini on the 7th may need conversation, movement, and mental exchange in partnership.

These are not fixed rules. They are starting points.

## Relationship Requires the Whole Chart

The 7th house is important, but relationship also involves Venus, the Moon, Mars, Saturn, the 5th house, and the condition of the chart ruler.

That is why compatibility alone is not enough. Your own chart shows what kind of relationship pattern you bring with you.
