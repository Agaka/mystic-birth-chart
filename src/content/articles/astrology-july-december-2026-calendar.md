The second half of 2026 is not one story. It contains overlapping cycles: Mercury revises Cancerian messages, Jupiter expands through Leo, Saturn and Neptune review early Aries, Uranus tests new Gemini systems, August brings eclipses, Pluto turns direct in Aquarius, and Venus and Mercury retrograde together through Scorpio season.

A calendar is useful only if it creates hierarchy. Major outer-planet stations describe collective background. Eclipses concentrate particular degrees. Personal relevance increases when a transit closely contacts a natal planet or angle, activates the profected house, or repeats a solar-return theme.

Dates below use the tropical geocentric zodiac. Stations can fall on adjacent calendar dates depending on timezone. Treat them as windows, not appointments with fate.

## July 2026: revision under an expanding Sun

July opens immediately after Jupiter's June 30 ingress into Leo. Creative growth, visibility, leadership, and confidence become major themes through mid-2027. Jupiter enlarges the Leo house but can also enlarge expense, pride, and performance pressure.

Mercury is retrograde in Cancer from June 29 to July 23, revisiting roughly 26 to 16 degrees Cancer. Family messages, home decisions, memory, security, and emotionally loaded agreements benefit from verification and a slower response. Read the full [Mercury retrograde in Cancer guide](/blog/mercury-retrograde-cancer-2026).

Neptune stationed retrograde near 4 degrees Aries on July 7. The station tests whether a new vision has accountable form. Inspiration needs a target, boundary, and evidence.

Around July 20, Jupiter near 4 degrees Leo opposes Pluto in Aquarius. Individual visibility meets the power of groups, platforms, and institutions. Leadership questions become inseparable from governance.

Mercury turns direct around July 23, but decisions may continue developing through the post-retrograde passage. Saturn stations retrograde near 15 degrees Aries on July 26, shifting emphasis from rapid initiative toward structural review.

**July practice:** revise the message, then revise the structure expected to carry it.

## August 2026: eclipses and changing visibility

The total solar eclipse near 20 degrees Leo on August 12 interrupts and renews themes of center, authorship, leadership, creativity, children, and honor. Jupiter's presence in Leo enlarges the field, but beginnings near an eclipse can contain unseen information.

The partial lunar eclipse near 5 degrees Pisces on August 28 culminates or releases a lunar pattern across the Virgo-Pisces axis. Method meets faith; distinction meets permeability; practical service meets compassion.

People with natal placements near 20 degrees of fixed signs or 5 degrees of mutable signs may experience the month more personally. House rulers and annual timing remain essential. Read [the August eclipse guide](/blog/august-2026-eclipses-leo-pisces) before making specific conclusions.

**August practice:** ask what deserves greater visibility and what must be released to support it.

## September 2026: debug the new network

Uranus stations retrograde near 6 degrees Gemini around September 10. Communication systems, learning tools, transport, interfaces, and networks introduced since spring may require debugging.

Uranus in Gemini rewards experimentation but can fragment attention. The retrograde phase asks whether novelty improved the function or merely accelerated it. People with early mutable placements may feel a stronger need to change plans, language, devices, study, or local movement.

Saturn and Neptune remain retrograde in Aries, so the larger question of vision and structure continues. The new system must have an owner, standard, and review process.

**September practice:** choose one communication system and document how information enters, changes, and leaves it.

## October 2026: value, power, and hidden terms

Venus stations retrograde near 8 degrees Scorpio on October 3. Trust, intimacy, shared money, desire, privacy, art, and value enter review. The cycle eventually returns to Libra, bringing hidden stakes back into explicit agreement.

Pluto turns direct near 3 degrees Aquarius around October 15. Group governance, technology, data, community power, and institutional design become more externally actionable after months of review.

Mercury stations retrograde near 21 degrees Scorpio around October 24. Its overlap with Venus makes documentation especially important. What is desired? What was promised? Who owns the resource? What remains private? What can each party actually consent to?

This is not a universal ban on contracts or relationships. It is a period for slower terms, professional advice where needed, and written records.

**October practice:** make hidden assumptions visible before calling an arrangement fair.

## November 2026: direct stations and negotiated form

Mercury turns direct near 5 degrees Scorpio around November 13. Venus turns direct near 23 degrees Libra on November 13 or 14 depending on timezone. The near-simultaneous stations create a strong turn in conversations about value, trust, money, aesthetics, and relationship.

Direct does not mean finished. Both planets need time to cross their reviewed degrees again. November favors decisions that acknowledge what the retrograde revealed rather than attempts to restore the exact previous arrangement.

Jupiter continues through Leo, supporting confidence and creative expansion while approaching its own December station. Watch overpromising as enthusiasm meets year-end limits.

**November practice:** write the revised agreement in language another person can actually evaluate.

## December 2026: structure, vision, and scale turn together

Saturn turns direct near 8 degrees Aries around December 10. Revised structures around action, leadership, responsibility, and boundaries can begin moving forward.

Around December 12, Neptune turns direct near 2 degrees Aries and Jupiter stations retrograde near 27 degrees Leo. This cluster joins three distinct processes. Saturn asks whether the structure is durable. Neptune asks whether it still serves the vision. Jupiter asks whether expansion remains meaningful and proportionate.

The answer may be a smaller but more coherent plan. Jupiter retrograde is not lost abundance; it is review of scale, confidence, teaching, creativity, and public generosity.

People with late fixed placements may begin Jupiter's repeated contacts; people with early cardinal placements remain inside the Saturn-Neptune reorganization.

**December practice:** decide what should continue, what needs a smaller scale, and what was only an inspiring image.

## The year's three major narratives

### 1. Center and system

Jupiter in Leo opposite Pluto in Aquarius and the Leo eclipse emphasize the relationship between individual visibility and collective infrastructure. Who receives attention? Who controls the platform? Does leadership create more agency or concentrate it?

### 2. Vision and structure

Saturn and Neptune in Aries began a new cycle in February and both retrograde in the second half of the year. The work is to give ideals accountable action without turning structure into an empty performance.

### 3. Value and language

Mercury retrograde in Cancer revises emotionally rooted messages. Venus and Mercury retrograde in Scorpio and Libra later revise trust, privacy, money, desire, and agreements. Clear language must remain connected with actual value.

## How to personalize the calendar

Start with your natal Ascendant and identify the houses containing Cancer, Leo, Aries, Gemini, Aquarius, Scorpio, Libra, and Pisces. Then find planets or angles near these zones:

- 16-26 degrees Cancer for the June-July Mercury cycle;
- early fixed signs for Jupiter opposite Pluto;
- 8-15 degrees cardinal signs for Saturn retrograde;
- 1-4 degrees cardinal signs for Neptune;
- 0-6 degrees mutable signs for Uranus;
- around 20 degrees fixed signs for the Leo eclipse;
- around 5 degrees mutable signs for the Pisces eclipse;
- 23 Libra through 8 Scorpio for Venus retrograde.

Next, calculate the [annual profection](/blog/annual-profections-time-lord-year). A transit to the lord of the year or activated house deserves more attention. Finally, compare the solar return for repeated planets, houses, and angles.

This hierarchy prevents a busy calendar from becoming generalized anxiety.

## What astrology can responsibly offer

Transit astrology can identify periods when particular functions become emphasized: review, expansion, restructuring, disruption, culmination, negotiation, or consolidation. It cannot replace medical, legal, financial, or mental-health expertise. It cannot guarantee political events or another person's behavior.

Use exact dates for planning attention and creating contingencies, not surrendering choice. Back up data during Mercurial reviews because backups are useful. Clarify agreements during Venus retrograde because clarity is ethical. Review scale during Jupiter's station because resources are finite.

The second half of 2026 contains enough motion to reward preparation, but not panic. The chart's purpose is to reveal which cycles belong to your life strongly enough to merit action. Everything else can remain weather in the distance.
