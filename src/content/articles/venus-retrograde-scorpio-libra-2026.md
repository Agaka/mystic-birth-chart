Venus stations retrograde near 8 degrees Scorpio on October 3, 2026, returns to Libra, and stations direct near 23 degrees Libra on November 13 or 14 depending on timezone. The cycle moves from questions of trust, intimacy, shared power, and hidden value back into Venus's own sign of agreement, reciprocity, fairness, and form.

Venus retrograde does not guarantee that an ex returns. It reviews how attraction becomes an agreement and what an agreement costs. Relationships are one expression; money, pricing, art, pleasure, aesthetics, and social bonds are others.

The movement from Scorpio to Libra suggests that intense attachment must return to explicit terms.

## The larger Venus cycle

Venus retrogrades about every nineteen months and traces a five-point pattern across the zodiac over eight years. The cycle includes a pre-retrograde passage, the station, an inferior conjunction with the Sun, the direct station, and a final passage through the reviewed degrees.

The first pass introduces the value question. Retrogradation returns to it. The conjunction with the Sun marks a seed or reorientation in the Venus cycle. The final pass implements new terms.

Look back roughly eight years for related themes in nearby zodiacal territory, but do not expect identical events. Natal context and the rest of the sky differ.

## Venus in Scorpio

Venus is traditionally in detriment in Scorpio, Mars's domicile. This does not mean people with Venus in Scorpio love badly. It means Venusian joining must operate under Mars's conditions: intensity, separation, risk, defense, and the need to confront what is hidden.

During the retrograde's Scorpio phase, questions can include exclusivity, jealousy, shared money, erotic trust, resentment, debt, inheritance, privacy, and the fear that vulnerability transfers power.

Mars's condition describes how these questions are handled. Clean Mars creates boundaries and directness. Distorted Mars uses threat, silence, or control. No astrological symbolism excuses coercion.

## Return to Libra

Venus returns to Libra, one of its domiciles, where it has more authority to negotiate, compare, balance, and make agreements. Yet retrograde Venus in dignity is still revising. The resources are available, but the terms are not finished.

Libra asks what each party gives, receives, and is permitted to decide. Harmony without explicit terms can conceal inequality. Fairness is not always identical treatment; it is proportionate and consensual arrangement.

The direct station in late Libra can mark a turn toward decision, but the final shadow passage remains important for implementation.

## Mercury retrograde overlaps

Mercury is also retrograde in Scorpio from around October 24 to November 13, 2026. The overlap intensifies review of language and value, especially around privacy, trust, contracts, money, and emotionally charged disclosures.

Venus asks what the bond is worth; Mercury asks what has actually been said and documented. This is a strong period for revising terms, recovering omitted information, and distinguishing attraction from agreement.

It is not automatically a bad time to sign. Some deadlines cannot move, and some renegotiations belong precisely to retrograde periods. Read carefully, seek legal or financial advice where appropriate, and record changes in writing.

## The Libra and Scorpio houses

Because Venus crosses two signs, read both natal houses as a sequence.

For an Aries Ascendant, Libra is the seventh and Scorpio the eighth: partnership terms lead into shared resources, trust, and intimacy. For a Taurus Ascendant, Libra is the sixth and Scorpio the seventh: work and routines connect with contracts, clients, and partnership. For a Libra Ascendant, the first and second houses connect identity with value and income.

The natal houses Venus rules, Taurus and Libra, remain active throughout the cycle. A Gemini Ascendant, for example, has Venus ruling the fifth and twelfth, connecting creativity, pleasure, children, romance, retreat, and hidden patterns with the current Libra-Scorpio houses.

This rulership network is why generic romance predictions are inadequate.

## Natal contacts

The retrograde zone extends approximately from 23 degrees Libra to 8 degrees Scorpio. Natal planets and angles in that interval receive repeated conjunctions. Late Aries and early Taurus receive oppositions; late Cancer and early Leo or late Capricorn and early Aquarius receive squares depending on degree.

Venus contacting natal Saturn may review commitment, price, scarcity, or relational limits. Contacting Jupiter can enlarge desire, generosity, or spending. Contacting Mars can intensify attraction and conflict. Contacting the Moon can revisit care, family patterns, and bodily comfort.

Repeated contacts unfold in phases. Do not force resolution on the first pass.

## Money and pricing

Venus governs value and exchange, so the retrograde can be useful for auditing subscriptions, debt agreements, client terms, pricing, and compensation. Scorpio asks who carries risk and who controls shared resources. Libra asks whether the exchange is proportionate.

Astrology is not financial advice. Use actual statements, contracts, tax rules, and professional counsel. The symbolic advantage of the period is attentional: hidden costs become part of the conversation.

Avoid using retrograde fear to create artificial scarcity or pressure purchases. Ethical Venus requires informed choice.

## Art, appearance, and old taste

Creative work may return for revision. An earlier aesthetic, collaborator, or unfinished piece can become relevant. Changes in appearance may reflect a deeper review of identity and value.

There is no universal prohibition against haircuts, cosmetic procedures, or design launches. For irreversible medical procedures, follow qualified advice and informed consent. For aesthetic choices, allow room for taste to change again after the direct station.

Retrograde Venus can recover beauty that was discarded too quickly, but nostalgia is not evidence that an old form still fits.

## A relationship audit without fatalism

Choose one important bond, romantic or otherwise. Write:

1. What each person has explicitly agreed to.
2. What each appears to expect without saying.
3. What resources, labor, privacy, or risk are shared.
4. What boundary would make reciprocity clearer.
5. What remains the other person's decision.

This exercise respects the Scorpio-Libra movement. It brings hidden stakes back into negotiable form.

Some relationships will deepen; some will change terms; some will end; many will simply become more honest. Venus retrograde predicts none of these alone. It creates a period in which the value structure is harder to ignore.

The cycle's best outcome is not reunion or perfect harmony. It is an agreement whose beauty can survive the truth about power, cost, and desire.
